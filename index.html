Hicks Emotional Guidance Scale

You don’t need to feel great.
You need to feel a little better.

A gentle map for moments when money feels uncertain, work feels unclear, and forcing positivity feels impossible.

↑Move one believable emotion at a time — not from fear straight to joy.
Tap any emotion for a believable next step

The aim is relief, not performance.

If the next thought feels fake, it is too far away. Choose a sentence your nervous system can actually believe. Even anger can be upward movement when you are coming from powerlessness.

01Name where you honestly are.
02Find the next softer thought.
03Take one small grounded action.
For reflective use, not as a substitute for financial, career, or mental-health support. The emotional sequence is attributed to Abraham–Hicks; examples and practices here are original adaptations.
